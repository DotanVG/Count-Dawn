Count Dawn

A free vampire survival browser game. You are the monster: hunt the waves of human hunters who came to kill you.

Collect enough blood to summon the night's leaders, defeat them, and return to your coffin before sunrise. Survive to face a harder night.

How to play

Desktop: Move with WASD or arrow keys. Aim with the mouse and click to strike. Shift is a bat dash; Space unleashes the Ultimate when Wrath is full. Esc or P pauses.

Touch: Move with the joystick. Tap toward a target to strike, or use the sword button to strike the nearest hunter. Use the bat button to dash, the lightning button for a charged Ultimate, and the pause button to pause.

Device guidance: Play in a browser with JavaScript enabled, using a keyboard and mouse or a touch screen. Touch gameplay requires landscape orientation; you can read this page in portrait.
